# League: Short description of the situation

- **Type:** Retrospective *(or Hypothetical)*
- **League:** 
- **Added:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Scenario

What happened, or what could happen, in two or three sentences. Include the game, the date, and the exact moment if it's a real event.

## Markets affected

Which contracts this touches: game winner, spread, total, player props, futures. Be specific about which ones are actually in question and which ones are clear.

## What the rules say

### Kalshi

Quote or summarize the relevant part of the market rules, and link to them.[^kalshi] Note anything vague or missing.

### Sportsbooks

How one or two major books handle the same situation, with links to their house rules.[^book] Say where they agree and where they differ.

### League

The league's own rule or stat definition, if it matters. Sometimes the edge case lives here and not in any market rule.[^league]

## What happened

*Retrospective entries only.* How the market was actually handled and settled, and when.

## My recommendation

!!! ruling "Clarification I would publish"
    Write this exactly as it would appear in a public market announcement. Plain language, one or two short paragraphs, no hedging.

## Reasoning

Why this is the right call. Consider fairness to both sides of the market, consistency with how similar situations have been settled, what traders would reasonably have expected when they entered, and how a market maker would read the rule before quoting.

If the existing rule already covers this well, say so and explain what it gets right. If it needs tighter wording, propose the wording.
