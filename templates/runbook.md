# Runbook: Event or day name

- **Covers:** Which leagues, markets, and time window
- **Updated:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Before the window opens

1. Confirm every scheduled market is listed with correct teams, times, and rule text.
2. Check injury reports, weather, and lineup news for anything that changes what should be listed.
3. Confirm settlement data feeds are live and agree with each other.

## During

### When to pause trading

The situations that trigger a pause, and the markets each one affects.

### When to reopen

What has to be confirmed, and from which sources.

### Escalation

What gets handled alone, what gets a second opinion, and who to bring in.

### Update wording

Fill-in-the-blank wording for the announcements that come up most often.

## After

1. Verify results against the official source before settling.
2. Note any market that needed a clarification, and whether the rule should change.
3. Watch for stat corrections until the correction window closes.
