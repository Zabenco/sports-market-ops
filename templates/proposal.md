## The market

What the contract asks, written as the yes/no question a trader would see.

## Why traders would disagree

Where the real uncertainty is. A market where everyone agrees on the answer won't trade.

## Structure

Contract type, strike levels if any, when it opens and closes, and why this framing beats the alternatives.

## Settlement source

The official data source, how fast results are available, and how corrections are handled.

## Edge cases

The situations the rules need to answer before listing, each with proposed wording.

## Risks

- **Demand and liquidity:** Is there an audience, and could a market maker price it?
- **Integrity:** Could the outcome be influenced or known early by insiders?
- **Workload:** What does live coverage require, and when does the event happen?

## Recommendation

The call and the main reason for it.
