# Casebook

Each entry takes one situation, lays out what the relevant rules say, and ends with the clarification I would publish and why.

Entries are one of two types:

**Retrospective** entries cover something that actually happened. They were written after the fact, so they include what Kalshi did.

**Hypothetical** entries cover situations that haven't come up yet but could. They focus on whether the current rules already answer the question.
